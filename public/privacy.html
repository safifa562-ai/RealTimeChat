Privacy Policy

Draft for launch review — last updated 17 October 2026.

This draft describes intended handling for the current RealTimeChat website. The owner must review it against the final deployed features, actual infrastructure, legal obligations and contact details before publishing.

Information handled

  • Account username, password hash and internal user ID.
  • Private and group messages, message metadata, friend requests and group membership stored to provide chat features.
  • Profile pictures and files users choose to upload.
  • Online status and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.
  • Notification history and certain settings may be stored in the browser's local storage.
  • If a user makes a Supporter payment, payment/order identifiers and supporter status are stored. Card/UPI details are handled by the configured payment provider; RealTimeChat should not store raw payment credentials.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, deliver messages and calls, display profiles, process account deletion, prevent abuse, troubleshoot reliability and record verified supporter purchases.

Storage and sharing

The current project uses Supabase for its database and file storage and may use Render for hosting and Razorpay for optional website checkout. Replace this statement if the production providers or settings change. Do not claim end-to-end encryption: the current application has not been independently validated as end-to-end encrypted.

Retention and deletion

Users can request permanent account deletion from the account menu. The owner should test that deletion removes profile data, messages, uploaded files and purchase records as described, while retaining only records legally required for transactions, security or dispute resolution.

Children and safety

Before inviting the public, the owner should decide and clearly state the minimum age and supervision requirements, publish reporting/blocking procedures, and comply with applicable child-safety and privacy requirements.

Your choices

Users should be able to update their profile, log out, control browser notification permissions and request account deletion. For privacy requests, contact the service owner using the support address published on the Support page.

Contact

Before public launch, replace the placeholder on the Support page with a real monitored support email or contact method and ensure it belongs to the responsible operator.

Do not publish this draft unchanged until a responsible adult/owner has reviewed it for the actual product and applicable law.
